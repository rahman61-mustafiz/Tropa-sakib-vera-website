About Us

A studio built for the women of Bashundhara.

Not another gym. A room designed around how you actually want to feel.

Where it started

We kept hearing the same three things.

Bashundhara has yoga clubs and gyms. What it didn't have was a space that felt truly private, truly premium, and truly flexible enough for a woman's actual week. So we built one, on Block C, right beside NSU.

Vera Wellness brings yoga, zumba and pilates into one studio for the first time in the neighbourhood. No mixed-gender floor to navigate, no beginner-intimidation, no rigid single-class schedule. Just a room that's entirely yours, four times a day, six days a week.

What we stand for

Four ideas, held to on purpose.

Privacy first

A women-only floor, shown through the space itself, not just a sign on the door.

No judgment

Beginners, mothers, first-timers, every class is built to welcome, not intimidate.

Real trainers

Certified, approachable, and genuinely invested in how you're doing.

A space worth the trip

Premium finishes, quiet corners, a room that feels like a small escape.

From the founder

Pilates, taught personally.

Pilates is still new to Dhaka, so I teach it myself, every session. It's the discipline I believe in most, and I wanted the first people to try it here to learn it properly, not from a trend clip.

Yoga and zumba are led by our two dedicated, certified trainers. Between the three of us, this studio is built to feel personal, not corporate, because that's the only version of a wellness studio I'd actually want to walk into myself.
